## Background & Context
AstraZit Music OS requires a definitive, platform-independent source of truth for its ~120 songs and future expansions. Following Codex adversarial audit review, the identity architecture was refined into an explicit tripartite separation between Musical Work (`AST-WRK`), Sound Recording (`AST-REC`), and Commercial Release (`AST-REL`), decoupling composition rights from master sound recording rights and establishing a distinct historical import policy.

## Technical Approach & Architecture Constraints
- **Identity Model**: Explicit namespaces (`AST-WRK-XXXXXX`, `AST-REC-XXXXXX`, `AST-REL-XXXXXX`). External platform IDs remain adapters.
- **Rights Model**: Composition rights attach to Work; master rights attach to Recording.
- **Historical Import Policy**: Native releases require approved rights. Historical imports allow `RELEASED` status while internal rights remain `PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`, without falsely asserting approval.
- **Radio Playout Target**: AstraZit Radio consumes eligible Recordings, not abstract works. Playout telemetry remains runtime-only.
- **Arithmetic Split Totals**: Decimal split summation to 100.0% is enforced deterministically in validation code.
