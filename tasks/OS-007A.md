# OS-007A — Safe Recovery of Incomplete Demo Sandbox

OS-007A closes the recovery dead-end left by an interrupted local demo
sandbox initialization. A marker with `initialized: false` remains unreadable
to summary, list, show, and validate operations. Destructive reset and
`init --force` may recover only a structurally verified incomplete DEMO
sandbox with the canonical mapping, expected root contents, and no symlink or
junction escapes. All other malformed or unexpected state fails closed.

No production allocator, Firestore backend, schema, or canonical repository
behavior is changed.
